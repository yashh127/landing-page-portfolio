Common Questions

Everything answered simply

I'm not technical at all. Can I update text and images myself later? +

Yes, absolutely! That’s why I offer Wix and Squarespace. Once your site is live, I record a friendly 5-minute video specifically for you, showing you exactly how to change words, upload new pictures, or check messages without needing any coding skills.

Do I need to have all the text and pictures ready before we start? +

Not at all. You just need a general idea of what you do. I help write the headlines, organize the sections, and provide high-quality royalty-free imagery that fits your brand.

How does payment work? +

To keep things fair and stress-free: we start with a simple conversation about what you need. For full sites, 50% is due once you approve the layout preview, and the remaining 50% is paid only when the site is fully complete and ready to launch.

Which platform should I choose: Wix, Squarespace, or Code? +

If you want complete visual freedom to drag items around, choose Wix. If you want an elegant, clean magazine-style look that's easy to maintain, choose Squarespace. If you want maximum speed and zero monthly platform fees, choose Custom Code. You can also try the AI helper above or ask me, and I’ll recommend what fits your budget best.
